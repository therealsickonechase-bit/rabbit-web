# Suggested wording for the privacy page (rabbitsoftware.io/privacy.html)

The public site currently says it has no accounts. Once sign-up opens, the privacy page must say what the
console collects. This is a starting draft in plain language, not legal advice. Have it reviewed for the places
your visitors live (for example GDPR in the EU/UK and CCPA in California) before sign-up opens.

---

## Accounts on the RabbitOS console

**What we collect.** When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Your password is
stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it. We also record when you created the account and when you
last signed in. If you use the summary feature, you give us your own Anthropic API key; we store it encrypted,
never show it back to you, and use it only to run summaries you ask for.

**What we use it for.** To run your account, send you the messages you ask for (confirming your email, resetting
your password), and keep the service secure.

**Contact record.** We also keep an encrypted copy of your email address as a contact record. It exists so that
we can respond to a valid legal request, such as a court order about illegal activity, and for no other purpose.
It is encrypted to a key held only by the site owner. We cannot read it in the course of running the service,
and each time one is opened we log it. If you delete your account, we keep the contact record for 12 months and
then remove it.

**Who else handles it.** Your account is stored with Supabase. Our messages to you are sent through Cloudflare.
Neither uses your address for its own marketing on our behalf.

**What members can see.** Members can read the public research library. Members cannot see the owner's personal
or sensor data, and cannot see other members.

**Your choices.** You can ask us to delete your account or correct your details by writing to
contact@rabbitsoftware.io.
